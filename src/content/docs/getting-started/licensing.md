Each extension uses a simple license activation. You activate once, and the tool keeps working offline after that.

## Activate

1. Open the panel in Premiere (**Window → Extensions → _(tool name)_**).
2. The first time it opens, you'll be asked for a **license key**. Paste the key from your purchase receipt.
3. That's it — the tool activates and is ready to use.

## What your license covers

- **One license per person.**
- **Up to 2 computers** that you personally use.
- **Works offline** after activation — you don't need to be online to keep using the tool once it's activated.

## Moving to a new computer

Replaced a machine, or need to move your license? If you hit the two-computer limit, email [sankarraknas7272@gmail.com](mailto:sankarraknas7272@gmail.com) and I'll reset your activations so you can activate on your new machine.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Activation failed" or "invalid key".** Double-check the key from your receipt for stray spaces, and make sure you're online for the initial activation. Copy-paste the key rather than typing it.
- **Hit the computer limit.** Email support to reset — see above.
