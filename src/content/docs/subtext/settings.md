Subtext is built to match Premiere and stay out of your way. Open settings from **☰ → Settings**.

## Appearance

- **Dark by default** to match Premiere.
- An optional **light theme**.
- A **customizable accent color** — pick the hue that suits you.
- A **UI-size** control that scales the whole panel, handy on high-resolution displays.

## General

- **On launch** — choose whether Subtext **reopens your previous session**, **starts fresh**, or **asks** each time.
- **Caption save location** — set your own folder for pushed/exported SRTs (defaults to a **Subtext** folder beside your project file).

## Privacy

Everything runs **locally** inside Premiere. Subtext edits caption files on your machine — **your files never leave your computer**.
