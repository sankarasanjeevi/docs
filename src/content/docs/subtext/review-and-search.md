Subtext has tools for reviewing a pass and making bulk text changes — without any of it leaking into your exported captions.

## Flag for review

- **Click the flag icon** on a caption to mark it. A **note field** appears where you can jot down why.
- Use the **flag button in the toolbar** to jump between flagged captions.
- Press **F** to flag the currently selected line.

Flags and notes are **Subtext-only** — they're for your review pass and never touch the exported SRT.

## Find & Replace

Open Find & Replace with the **magnifier** button or **Ctrl+F**.

- Matches are **highlighted as you type**.
- **Enter** / **Shift+Enter** step through matches.
- **Replace all** changes every match at once.
- Toggle **Aa** for case-sensitive matching.
- Toggle **W** for whole-word matching — so "at" won't match inside "procrastinating".
