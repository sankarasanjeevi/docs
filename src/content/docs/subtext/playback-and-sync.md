Subtext stays in step with Premiere's playhead so you can check timing without leaving the panel.

## Playback

- The **Play / Stop** button, or the **Spacebar**, toggles timeline playback.
- **Single-click any caption** to jump Premiere's playhead to that caption.

## Sync mode

Turn on **Sync** to highlight the caption **under the playhead** as the sequence plays. As Premiere plays through your timeline, Subtext follows along and keeps the current caption highlighted — so you can watch and read at the same time and catch timing problems in real time.
