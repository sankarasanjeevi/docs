Version history for **Subtext**.

## 1.0.0 — first release

- **Windows and macOS** — one package installs on both.
- **SRT, VTT and CSV import**, plus whole-folder import. A CSV with frame timecodes asks for your frame
  rate once.
- **Word-level editing**, click-to-seek and Sync mode, flags with notes, undo and redo, and
  auto-saved sessions.
- **Add and delete captions** anywhere in the list.
- **Cleanup bar** — remove gaps, line breaks, and punctuation across the whole file, all undoable.
- **Find & Replace** across all captions.
- **In / Out push scope** — push everything or only the sequence's In/Out range. Pushed SRTs are
  filed in a **Subtext** bin.
- **License activation** — one license, up to 2 computers, works offline after activation.
- Optional **light theme**, a customizable **accent color**, and a **UI-size control** that scales
  the whole panel.
- Keyboard flow: Up/Down walk the captions with the playhead following, Enter edits, F flags.
